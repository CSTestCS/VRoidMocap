How it works

  1. Analysis scans every frame once: finds camera cuts, tracks every person, recognises recurring characters (even after they leave the frame or the shot changes), measures their height against the scenery and solves the camera angle, tilt and lens for each frame.
  2. Pick a character. Motion capture runs a second, high-accuracy pass on just that character (body, hands, fingers and feet) and uses the analysis data for depth, scale and camera angles.
  3. Watch the result from the video's camera or a free camera, scrub, lock movement in place, and export .fbx. Pick another character any time without re-analysing.
